Walkability & Nearby Destinations

Taniti City is fairly flat and very walkable. Many visitors stay near Merriton Landing, an area that is easy to explore on foot.

Taniti City

Taniti City is fairly flat and very walkable, making it easy to explore the area on foot.

Merriton Landing

Many tourists stay around Merriton Landing, where many entertainment options are located and the area is easy to explore on foot.

Beaches

Taniti City is located near the white, sandy beaches surrounding Yellow Leaf Bay.

Restaurants & Entertainment

Many restaurants and entertainment options are available around Taniti City and Merriton Landing. Check the map to plan your route.
