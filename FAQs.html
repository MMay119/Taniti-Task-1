Frequently Asked Questions

What currency is used on Taniti?

The currency used on Taniti is the Tanitian dollar. U.S. dollars are also accepted at many locations.

What language is spoken on Taniti?

English is commonly spoken on Taniti. Visitors should have little difficulty communicating with residents.

What is the weather like on Taniti?

Taniti has a tropical climate with warm temperatures throughout most of the year.

How can I get around Taniti?

Visitors can travel around the island by rental car, taxi, bus, bicycle, or other available transportation.

Are there hospitals or medical clinics on Taniti?

Yes. Taniti has medical facilities available for visitors and residents who need medical assistance.

Do I need a passport to visit Taniti?

Visitors should check the entry requirements that apply to their country before traveling to Taniti.

What activities are available for visitors?

Taniti offers a variety of activities including beaches, rainforest excursions, sightseeing, dining, and other outdoor activities.

Where can I find maps of Taniti?

Maps and visitor information can be found on our Maps page.
